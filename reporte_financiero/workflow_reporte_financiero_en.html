Design notes
  • PENDINGNursery alignment: The written SOP only lists Tomás (Food Production / Animal Production / Biofactory) and Javier (Ganadería) — Landscaping was the third subdepartment named at the time. Since then, Landscaping moved to Experiences and the Nursery moved from Operations to Farm. This map assumes the Nursery follows the same process, mirrored from the other lanes, but that has not been confirmed with Daniel yet. Validate the actual process with him before treating this lane as final.
  • Landscaping excluded: The SOP document still names Alejandro Alvarado / Paisajismo as in scope, but Landscaping was recently reassigned from Farm to Experiences. This map reflects the current org structure, not the document as written, so Landscaping is intentionally left out.
  • Internal transaction matching: Every Comprobante Interno (TI-XXX) is logged twice — as income by the providing subdepartment and as an expense by the receiving one — and the two entries must reconcile at month-end (same TI number, same amount).
  • Receiver outside Farm: When the receiving party is a non-Farm department (e.g. Kitchen), the Farm subdepartment still logs the income side and sends the comprobante, but the receiving department logs its own expense in its own system — that side isn't represented in this map.
  • Deadlines: Each subdepartment lead must email their monthly report to finca@tierramor.cr by day 15 of the following month. Javier reviews all reports and consolidates the department-wide report by day 20.
  • CAPEX: Capital purchases (equipment, infrastructure, anything with a useful life beyond one year) still go through the same Registro de Egresos, but require prior approval from the General Manager before the purchase is made — not shown as a separate path here.
